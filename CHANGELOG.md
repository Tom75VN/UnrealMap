## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-27

### Added

- World map: minimize it to a small window you can move and resize while you keep playing.
- World map: smooth zoom with + and - or the mouse wheel, with a "Zoom" hint; drag and fling to look around the zone, which stays centred.
- World map: zoom in on a zone to fade smoothly into its real 3D terrain, up to 14x.
- World map: every classic zone now has the themed unexplored look, not just five.
- Settings: the Packs page shows the installed version of each data pack.
- Data packs: update unrealMap_World to 1.1.0 for the terrain view.
- Data packs: update unrealMap_Unexplored to 1.0.0 for the new unexplored zones.

### Changed

- World map: with Unreal UI installed, its zone names and cursor coordinates stay on the map.
- Support popup: answer "I already liked it" to stop it, or "OK, I will add a like".
- Support popup: shown side by side with other Unreal addons' popups instead of on top.
- Support popup: clearer wording.
